---
description: Where a client's month-end close stands, in one paragraph
argument-hint: <client>
---

Call the Pursor `close` tool for the client named in `$ARGUMENTS` (if none
is named, call `clients` first and ask which). Then say, in one plain
paragraph: which day of the close it is, how many rows are done, and what
stands in the way — the cards and journals still open, by step. If the
month is signed, say who signed it and when, and whether the period is
still open in the ledger. Name the
step and the count; do not list every row unless asked. End with the
page's link.
